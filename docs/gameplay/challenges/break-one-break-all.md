# Minecraft Game Mode: Breaking a Block BREAKS THEM ALL

## Core Mechanics
The rule, stated outright at the start: "you break a block, that specific block will get broken across the entire Minecraft world." Breaking one block destroys every block of that *same type* everywhere — not just loaded chunks. Breaking grass removes all grass; breaking one birch log removes all birch logs ("now we don't have any more birch logs"). The broken blocks drop their normal items as entities, but because hundreds drop at once and scatter, the players race to grab them before they despawn. The effect is type-specific: cobblestone is treated as distinct from stone, deepslate distinct from stone, and netherrack, gravel, quartz, end stone, iron bars, spawners, etc. each chain independently. Played in hardcore by two players (Caleb and a partner).

## Impact on Gameplay
- **Resources arrive in bulk but vanish fast:** one coal ore yields a "stack of 21," one iron node "50 iron" — but loose drops despawn, so they pre-mark veins before breaking.
- **Food becomes scarce:** breaking one pig makes "all the pigs extinct"; mobs and animals don't respawn, forcing reliance on apples farmed by breaking all oak leaves at once.
- **Structural blocks are deadly:** breaking stone, dirt, or netherrack risks wiping the world out from under you and exposing every cave and hostile mob simultaneously.
- **Explosions are catastrophic:** creepers and especially ghast fireballs break blocks too, deleting entire nether-rack paths in one shot ("all the netherrack gone").
- **Constant lag and crashes:** mass block updates freeze games, kick players, and cause near-deaths.

## Notable Strategies
- **Mark before breaking:** they manually note ore locations so they can collect the flood of drops before despawn.
- **Harvest by side effect:** breaking oak leaves for apples; looting a bastion by breaking it; opening end-crystal cages by breaking one iron bar to clear them all.
- **Avoid the pickaxe on obsidian/lava:** they crafted buckets but accidentally removed *all* lava at once; they recover via natural obsidian and a ruined portal.
- **Strip the ceiling for visibility:** breaking the top block at the bedrock layer to see the stronghold while traveling.

## Endgame Adaptation
They reach the Nether (fighting ghasts, looting a bastion, grinding natural gold and blazes after a partner broke a blaze spawner), then the stronghold, then the End. The mode reaches the dragon fight: a player breaks one iron bar to open all end-crystal cages, but the dragon itself breaks their placed and end-stone blocks mid-flight ("the dragon should not be able to break our blocks"). Despite one death over the void, they kill the dragon and win.

## Memorable Moments
- A partner accidentally broke a gold block, dropping every bastion gold block into the lava.
- Breaking a blaze spawner deleted blaze rod farming, forcing natural blaze grinding.
- Dumping a bucket removed all overworld lava, stranding them from the Nether.
- A ghast fireball erased a netherrack path; standing on the fortress saved them.
- Final win after a void death, ending back at a (still-living) village.
