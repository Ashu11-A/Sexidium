# Minecraft Game Mode: Chained Together (Multiplayer)

## Core Mechanics
Five players (host "C Ravioli" plus four friends — Fish, Xenas/Zenith, Blue/Gray Blue, and one more) are all tethered together using the "Chained to Your Friends" mod. The chain is anchored to an invisible shared center point ("There's like a center point that we can't see"); nobody can exceed the maximum leash distance from it, so when everyone walks in different directions, "nothing's going to happen" and the group stays stuck. The visible chains are a per-screen visual bug ("everybody's screen is going to look like everyone is chained to them"). The chain physics are jank: jumping or hitting another player flings people around ("if you jump, it like moves you... if you hit somebody else, they just die"). Layered on top via a Deathlink datapack is a **death link** — "if one person dies" the whole team dies. The host can teleport players to himself, but one misfire ("meant to teleport at A and I teleported at E") yanked every entity at once. Crucially, catching the Ender Dragon's **dragon's breath** in a bottle dissolves the chains — and "in theory, death link is also off now" too.

## Impact on Gameplay
- **Movement is a democracy:** Even grabbing "a couple logs" needs the whole group to agree and walk together ("a democratic process to get the freaking logs").
- **Death link raises the stakes:** A single mob is terrifying ("there's one zombie. We are screwed"); one careless player kills everyone.
- **Friendly fire is lethal:** Mistimed attacks delete teammates ("Zenith fish, YOU KILLED ZENITH?").
- **No solo work:** Nobody can split off — "one of us can't just go and grab a couple logs right now."
- **Foxy jump-scare mod:** A 1-in-10,000-per-second random jump scare adds extra chaos.

## Notable Strategies
- **"Flow state":** Everyone running the same direction at once moves smoothly — and even lets them scale walls without building.
- **Designated leader callouts:** "Follow C Ravioli" — go whichever way he goes — plus Overwatch-style calls ("Focus cows," "Flank the creeper to the right").
- **Run, don't fight:** Agreeing to actually flee mobs together instead of panicking in circles.
- **Block off threats / bunker up:** Trapping an Enderman in a boat, building a house around their first lava pool.
- **Shared bed/spawn:** Break all beds but one so everyone respawns together.

## Endgame Adaptation
They reached the **Nether** (chaos getting the team through the portal, a wither-skeleton fight, blaze rods farmed up to 8), then hunted Endermen for pearls ("12 eyes of ender now. 13") using a block-while-aggro'd tactic. The stronghold was absurdly deep ("how many layers is this?... is this Silent Hill 2?"). At the **End** they bottled the dragon's breath to **break the chains and disable death link**, then fought "a normal end fight" and finally killed the dragon — **they beat the game** after 3.5 hours.
