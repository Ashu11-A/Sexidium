# Minecraft Game Mode: Jumping Enchants Things

## Core Mechanics
Every time the player jumps, a **random item in their inventory** receives a **random enchantment**. The player controls neither which item is picked nor which enchantment lands. Enchantments are not limited to vanilla-appropriate gear: dirt, wood, cobblestone, wool, pork chops, and even ender pearls all get enchanted. Repeated rolls stack the same enchantment to absurd levels (Fortune, Protection 15+, Sharpness 16, Lunge, Curse of Vanishing 36), pushing values "into number territory." Not every enchantment functions on its odd host item ("It doesn't seem like all of the enchantments are working, but some of them definitely are"), and harmful ones (curses, runaway Lunge) can appear without warning.

## Impact on Gameplay
- **OP from junk:** A leather helmet with respiration/oxygen bonus and a single wool block become more powerful than diamond gear.
- **Inventory chaos:** Nothing stacks because every item is individually enchanted, forcing heavy inventory management.
- **Lunge is double-edged:** A "Lunge" enchant launches the player into enemies, causing fire and fall problems and knocking them into hazards.
- **Saturation drain:** Each high-level lunge burns saturation fast, gating healing and sprinting and turning fights into hunger-vs-kill math.
- **Unpredictable black box:** Untriggered enchantments lurk on gear and can fire dangerously at any moment.

## Notable Strategies
- **Bundle insulation:** Crafts nested bundles and stores wanted tools inside so the **bundle** absorbs the enchant instead of the tool, protecting good gear.
- **Spam jumping:** Stands still mashing space to grind enchantment levels onto chosen items (wool, helmet).
- **Godwool weapon:** Concentrates rolls onto a wool block, building a Sharpness 16 / Protection one-hit "super wool."
- **Aquaman build:** Uses a respiration helmet for underwater mining and breathing instead of chasing diamonds.
- **Shift safety:** Discovers shifting still lunges but prevents walking off edges; lunging in water is suppressed.

## Endgame Adaptation
Worried that uncontrolled Lunge will fling him into the void, the player tests whether enchanted Eyes of Ender still activate the portal (they do) and uses a boosted, Curse-of-Vanishing-36 ender pearl to travel. In the Nether he gathers blaze rods and bones while shielding his dog. In the End, the godwool deals huge damage to the dragon, but swinging repeatedly launches him "into the sky," nearly into the void; feather falling saves him. He dies once, recovers his stuff, and finally beats the Ender Dragon with the wool.

## Memorable Moments
- "Wind burst one dirt" and Infinity/Flame dirt as the first absurd enchants.
- An enchanted pork chop with Infinity that does nothing when tested.
- Protection 24 / Pro 29 / Sharpness 16 godwool one-hitting mobs.
- Curse of Vanishing 36 — "banished 36 times into the shadow realm."
- Obsessively protecting Jeb the dog; multiple Jebs lost, including one yeeting itself off a ledge into lava.
- Swinging at the dragon and "disappearing into the sky," surviving on one heart, dying, then returning to finish for Jeb's sake.
