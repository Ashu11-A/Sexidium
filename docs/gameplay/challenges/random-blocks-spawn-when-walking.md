# Minecraft Game Mode: Random Blocks Spawn When You Walk

## Core Mechanics
Every time the player walks over a block, a random block spawns beneath their feet. As Wisp (the YouTuber) states up front: "every time you walk over a block a random block spawns below your feet, this could be any block from sand to a block of netherite." The replacement is fully random across the entire block palette, including dragon eggs, emerald blocks, diamond blocks, TNT, ancient debris, netherite blocks, water, sponge, and spawners. The stated challenge is to beat the Ender Dragon "without destroying the world." Because the player constantly leaves a trail of randomized blocks behind them, loot accumulates simply by moving. This is a Minecraft 1.16 challenge, not a speedrunner-vs-hunter format.

## Impact on Gameplay
- **No mining required:** Wood, food, iron, and even diamond blocks appear by walking, so the player "[doesn't] even have to mine trees" and gets armor materials passively.
- **Constant sinking hazard:** Blocks change underfoot, repeatedly trapping the player ("I'm stuck," "I'm gonna sink into the world," "let me out").
- **Jumping deletes ground:** Jumping makes the blocks below disappear, so the player must avoid jumping to keep footing.
- **Surfaces glitch movement:** Soul sand and snow are extra glitchy because each counts as a block, though walking normally on soul sand is "safe."
- **Block-destruction risk:** Breaking key structure blocks (end portal frames, spawners, fortresses) must be avoided so as not to ruin progress.

## Notable Strategies
- **Stay still / don't jump:** Holding position and refusing to jump preserves the floor during combat and climbs.
- **Break-for-loot:** Breaking an ender chest yields obsidian; breaking and using a smithing table upgrades every diamond tool to netherite.
- **Trap piglins for trading:** Wisp herds piglins into an enclosure and tosses gold in to farm ender pearls safely, without breaking the surrounding blocks.
- **Water-bucket clutch:** Saves spawned water (including water that appears in the Nether) as a fall/launch safety net.
- **Build portals from outside:** Places obsidian and frames from a distance to avoid sinking into lava or breaking the structure.

## Endgame Adaptation
This is a full playthrough. Wisp reaches near-full netherite gear just by walking, then carefully builds a nether portal. He survives a lava-heavy Nether to farm blaze rods from a fortress and a blaze spawner, traps piglins for ender pearls, and uses eyes of ender to locate the stronghold. He avoids standing on the end portal frames, builds up around the portal, sets his spawn, and enters the End.

## Memorable Moments
- An ancient debris / netherite block appearing almost immediately, which he calls "ridiculously overpowered."
- Repeatedly getting stuck and nearly dying beside lava ("let me out, let me out").
- Spawning water in the Nether: "we've broken the game."
- A sponge spawning at the worst possible moment, then becoming a water-clutch system.
- Killing the dragon with TNT and a Power IV bow, briefly losing his chestplate, then crafting a diamond one mid-fight to survive.
